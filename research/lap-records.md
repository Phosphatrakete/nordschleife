# Nürburgring Nordschleife Lap Records — Status as of mid-2026

Research date: 1 July 2026. Nothing below is invented; conflicts between sources are
flagged. **Track-length note:** the full Nordschleife lap used for official record
attempts is **20.832 km** (flying start, timed at the T13 start/finish line).
Historically, manufacturers often quoted times on the **~20.6 km** lap (omitting the
T13 straight), so older times are not directly comparable. Since 2019 the Nürburgring
itself officially certifies and lists record laps
([Record Drives — nuerburgring.de](https://nuerburgring.de/info/nuerburgring/records?locale=en)).

## 1. All-time outright lap record

- **5:19.546** — **Timo Bernhard**, **Porsche 919 Hybrid Evo** (modified LMP1),
  **29 June 2018**, 20.832 km, average 233.8 km/h. Still unbeaten as of mid-2026.
- Sources: [Porsche Newsroom](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/motorsports/porsche-919-hybrid-evo-record-nuerburgring-nordschleife-5-minutes-19-seconds-55-timo-bernhard-15752.html),
  [Autocar](https://www.autocar.co.uk/car-news/motorsport-le-mans/porsche-919-evo-nurburgring-lap-record),
  [Nürburgring official news](https://nuerburgring.de/news/rekordjagd-auf-der-nordschleife-die-offiziellen-bestzeiten-2025?locale=en)

## 2. The previous legendary record (Bellof qualifying lap)

- **6:11.13** — **Stefan Bellof**, **Porsche 956** (Rothmans works car), **28 May 1983**,
  qualifying for the Nürburgring 1000 km (World Sportscar Championship), avg ~202 km/h.
  Stood for 35 years until Bernhard's 2018 lap.
- Sources: [Porsche Newsroom (Christophorus)](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/christophorus/porsche-christophorus-stefan-bellof-nuerburgring-record-12756.html),
  [stefan-bellof.de](https://www.stefan-bellof.de/en-us/karriere/rekordrunde/),
  [Wikipedia — Stefan Bellof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Bellof)

## 3. Fastest lap in actual race conditions

- **6:25.91** — **Stefan Bellof**, **Porsche 956**, during the **1983 1000 km
  Nürburgring race**. Still the fastest race-condition lap (2018/2019 runs were solo
  record attempts). *Conflict flag: most sources cite 6:25.91; a few German sources
  render 6:25.19.*
- Sources: [Porsche 956 — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche_956),
  [Motorsport-Total](https://www.motorsport-total.com/wec/news/vor-35-jahren-bellof-und-der-unfassbare-nordschleifen-rekord-18052801)

## 4. Production / road-legal car record

- **6:29.090** — **Maro Engel**, **Mercedes-AMG ONE**, **23 September 2024**, 20.832 km,
  notarized. First road car under 6:30. **Still the production-car record mid-2026.**
- Two faster 2025–26 laps do NOT count as production records:
  Xiaomi SU7 Ultra **prototype** 6:22.091 (non-production, see below); **Ford GT Mk IV**
  6:15.977 — Frédéric Vervisch, **1 April 2026** — track-only (not road-legal), fastest
  pure-ICE lap ever recorded at the 'Ring.
- Sources: [Mercedes-Benz media](https://media.mbusa.com/releases/release-9ce9755cd9dcbb00611364b99f035b12-629090-min-mercedes-amg-one-breaks-its-own-lap-record-on-the-nurburgring-nordschleife),
  [Autocar](https://www.autocar.co.uk/car-news/new-cars/mercedes-amg-one-takes-nurburgring-production-car-lap-record),
  [Auto Express — fastest 'Ring laps](https://www.autoexpress.co.uk/best/fastest-nurburgring-lap-record-times),
  [Ford — GT Mk IV](https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/articles/2026/ford-gt-mk-iv-nurburgring-record)

## 5. Electric-vehicle records

- **Outright EV:** **6:05.336** — **Romain Dumas**, **Volkswagen ID.R** (500 kW electric
  prototype), **3 June 2019**. Second-fastest lap of any kind. Still stands mid-2026.
  Source: [Volkswagen Newsroom](https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/en/press-releases/605336-minutes-volkswagen-idr-sets-new-electric-record-on-the-nuerburgring-5045)
- **Fastest production EV (mid-2026):** **6:55.553** — **Lars Kern**, **Porsche Taycan
  Turbo GT with Manthey Kit** (Weissach Package), **15 April 2026**, notarized
  ("Electric Executive Cars" category; fastest lap by any production EV). *Flag: one
  Porsche headline printed "06:55.533"; body text and all media report 6:55.553.*
  Sources: [Porsche Newsroom](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2026/products/porsche-taycan-turbo-gt-manthey-kit-record-nordschleife-42424.html),
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/05/07/porsche-taycan-turbo-gt-manthey-kit-nurburgring-ev-record/)
- Recent lineage: **Yangwang U9 Xtreme (BYD)** 6:59.157, Moritz Kranz, **22 Aug 2025**
  (first EV under 7:00; "electric super sports car" category)
  ([Nürburgring official](https://www.nuerburgring.de/news/neuer-rekord-fuer-den-yangwang-u9-xtreme-das-schnellste-elektrische-supersportauto-auf-der-nordschleife?locale=en));
  **Xiaomi SU7 Ultra** (production) 7:04.957, Vincent Radermecker, **1 April 2025**
  ([Nürburgring official](https://nuerburgring.de/news/rekordrunde-auf-der-nordschleife-xiaomi-su7-ultra-ist-mit-7-04-957-minuten-schnellstes-e-fahrzeug-der-oberklasse?locale=en));
  Porsche Taycan Turbo GT 7:07.55 (Lars Kern, 2023).

## 6. Category records

- **Fastest SUV (production):** **7:22.755** — **Xiaomi YU7 GT** with Track Professional
  Package (electric, 990 hp), May 2026. Earlier pre-launch test-vehicle lap: 7:34.931.
  Previous: Audi RS Q8 Performance 7:36.698 (2024); Porsche Cayenne Turbo GT 7:38.925
  (2021). The YU7 GT also set the first officially timed **autonomous** lap
  (10:29.483, 8 June 2026, no occupants).
  Sources: [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-news/suvs/xiaomi-yu7-gt-has-smashed-suv-record-nurburgring),
  [CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2026/05/19/xiaomi-yu7-gt-breaks-nurburgring-suv-lap-record-with-734-931-ahead-of-may-21-launch/),
  [Electrek (autonomous lap)](https://electrek.co/2026/06/22/xiaomi-yu7-gt-sets-another-nurburgring-record-with-nobody-in-the-drivers-seat/)
- **Fastest sedan / 4-door (production):** the Taycan Turbo GT Manthey (6:55.553, April
  2026). Non-production footnote: **Xiaomi SU7 Ultra prototype** 6:22.091, David
  Pittard, April 2025 (third-fastest officially certified time ever)
  ([Nürburgring official](https://nuerburgring.de/news/xiaomi-su7-ultra-prototype-absolviert-eine-rundenzeit-von-6-22-091-minuten-auf-der-nordschleife?locale=en)).
- **Fastest front-wheel-drive (production):** **7:44.523** — **Benjamin Leuchter**,
  **VW Golf GTI EDITION 50** (325 PS), May 2026; beat the Honda Civic Type R (FL5)
  7:44.881 (April 2023) by 0.358 s.
  Sources: [Volkswagen Newsroom](https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/en/press-releases/golf-gti-edition-50-is-the-fastest-frontwheel-drive-production-model-on-the-nuerburgring-nordschleife-20365),
  [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-news/hot-hatch/take-honda-vw-has-snatched-front-wheel-drive-nurburgring-record)
- **Fastest manual-transmission production car:** **6:56.294** — **Jörg Bergmeister**,
  **Porsche 911 GT3 (992.2)** 6-speed manual with Weissach Package, announced
  **17 April 2025**, notarized. Beat the Dodge Viper ACR's 7:01.3 (2017, ~20.6 km lap).
  Source: [Porsche Newsroom](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2025/products/porsche-911gt3-nordschleife-nuerburgring-39264.html)

## 7. Motorcycle record

- **7:49.71** — **Helmut Dähne**, **Honda RC30 (VFR750R)**, **23 May 1993**, full lap.
  Effectively "eternal": motorcycle homologation for Nordschleife racing lapsed after
  1994. Source: [Helmut Dähne — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmut_D%C3%A4hne)

## 8. Nürburgring 24 Hours context (24h layout = 25.378 km)

- **Distance record:** 162 laps / 4,111.24 km — Frikadelli Racing **Ferrari 296 GT3**,
  **2023**. Source: [24h-rennen.de](https://www.24h-rennen.de/en/the-legend/)
- **2026 race (16–17 May):** Max Verstappen (Mercedes-AMG GT3) set a GT3
  race-conditions lap record of **7:51.514** during a night stint but retired; the #80
  Mercedes-AMG of Engel/Stolz/Schiller/Martin won.
  Sources: [Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/NLS/news/analysis-how-dominant-was-max-verstappen-at-the-nurburgring-24-hours/10822585/),
  [GPFans](https://www.gpfans.com/en/f1-news/1083844/max-verstappen-nurburgring-24-hours-mercedes-amg-race-results-final-times/)
- GT3 reference on the pure Nordschleife: Christian Krognes, BMW M4 GT3, **7:49.578**
  (2022, NLS conditions).

## Official record-attempt rules (Nürburgring GmbH, since 2019)

- Full 20.832 km lap, flying start, timed at the official T13 start/finish line.
- A notary supervises every attempt; timing uses calibrated equipment; TÜV Rheinland
  verifies the vehicle's production conformity.
- Source: [Record Drives — nuerburgring.de](https://nuerburgring.de/info/nuerburgring/records?locale=en)

## Open flags / caveats

1. Bellof race lap: 6:25.91 (dominant) vs 6:25.19 (minority of sources).
2. Taycan Manthey time: 6:55.553 everywhere except one Porsche headline ("06:55.533").
3. Xiaomi YU7 GT: two times published in May 2026 (7:34.931 test car; 7:22.755
   production with track package).
4. "Fastest production EV" category naming differs between the Ring's classes; on raw
   time the Taycan Manthey (6:55.553) is quickest as of mid-2026.
5. nuerburgring.de and Wikipedia could not be fetched directly from the build
   environment; official-page details rest on search-index excerpts plus manufacturer
   press releases.
