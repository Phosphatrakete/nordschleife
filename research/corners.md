# Nürburgring Nordschleife — Corner-by-Corner Research

Research date: 1 July 2026. Compiled from web sources with attribution; where sources
conflict this is noted. The official Nürburgring "sections of the track" page
([nuerburgring.de](http://www.nuerburgring.de/en/fans-info/race-tracks/nordschleife/sections-of-the-track.html))
exists but could not be fetched directly from the build environment.

## Corner count

- **Official count: 73 corners — 33 left, 40 right** (post-1970/71 configuration).
  Sources: [ADAC NLS — The Nürburgring](https://www.nuerburgring-langstrecken-serie.de/en/the-nurburgring/),
  [Oversteer48 corner names guide](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/),
  [GetSpeed — famous corners](https://www.getspeed-racetaxi.de/en/blog-artikel/the-most-famous-corners-on-the-nurburgring----and-their-stories)
- Depending on definition, counts range up to ~150–170 directional changes; about
  40+ named sections cover the 73 official corners.
  Sources: [nring.info corner map](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/),
  [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)
- Elevation extremes: ~616.8 m near Hohe Acht (highest), ~320 m at Breidscheid
  (lowest); max incline 17%, max descent 11%.
  Sources: [Nordschleife-BtG — Gradient of altitude](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/nordschleife/gradient-of-altitude/),
  [Big Garage](https://www.biggarage.de/info/nuerburgring.html),
  [climbfinder](https://climbfinder.com/en/climbs/hohe-acht-nurburgring-nordschleife-breidscheid)

## Sections in track order (from modern start/finish, T13)

### Sabine-Schmitz-Kurve
Left-hander, first Nordschleife corner after the GP-circuit junction (between
Hohenrain and Hatzenbach). Officially named September 2021 for Sabine Schmitz, "Queen
of the Nordschleife", first woman to win the N24 — only the third renaming in track
history. Sources: [nuerburgring.de](https://nuerburgring.de/news/besondere-ehre-fur-die-botschafterin-des-nurburgrings-nordschleife-bekommt-sabine-schmitz-kurve),
[ADAC NLS](https://www.nuerburgring-langstrecken-serie.de/language/de/2021/06/18/nordschleife-bekommt-sabine-schmitz-kurve/),
[nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/sabine-schmitz-kurve/)

### Hatzenbach
Twisting right-left-right S-complex — highest corner concentration on the track,
entered via the fast Hatzenbach-Bogen. Name: "hatzen" (to hunt) + "Bach" (brook).
Classic early-lap accident spot. Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/hatzenbach/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/hatzenbach-and-hocheichen/)

### Hocheichen
Long right into a tricky downhill-then-uphill left. Name: "High Oaks" — for oaks that
stood left of the track. Sources: [Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/hatzenbach-and-hocheichen/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)

### Quiddelbacher Höhe
Flat-out uphill crest/jump where the track crosses a bridge; cars go light. Named for
the high point near Quiddelbach. Resurfaced with Flugplatz after 2015.
Sources: [Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-3-drone/),
[Oversteer48 — jumps](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-jump/),
[BridgeToGantry](https://www.bridgetogantry.com/flugplatz-your-jump-is-safe-for-now/)

### Flugplatz
High-speed sweeping right with blind entry over a crest; famously airborne. Name:
"Airfield" — a small glider airfield lay beside the track. Incident: March 2015 VLN,
Jann Mardenborough's Nissan GT-R GT3 went over the fence, killing a spectator —
prompted fencing changes and resurfacing.
Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/flugplatz/),
[RACER](https://racer.com/2015/03/28/spectator-killed-in-nurburgring-crash),
[Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/NLS/news/top-stories-of-2015-15-fatal-nordschleife-crash-prompts-safety-changes/664558/)

### Schwedenkreuz
Very fast, slightly banked left approached at 200+ km/h over a crest; widely called
the most dangerous corner. Name: "Swedish Cross" — a stone cross commemorating Adenau
tax collector Hans Friedrich Datenberg, murdered by Swedish soldiers in 1638 (Thirty
Years' War). Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/schwedenkreuz/),
[BridgeToGantry](https://www.bridgetogantry.com/video-is-schwedenkreuz-the-most-dangerous-corner-on-the-nurburgring/),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/schwedenkreuz-and-aremberg/)

### Aremberg
~90° medium-speed right at the bottom of the hill, leading onto the drop into the
Fuchsröhre. Named after the nearby Aremberg hill/castle/village.
Sources: [Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/schwedenkreuz-and-aremberg/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)

### Fuchsröhre
Plunging downhill left-right sequence into a violent compression, then steep uphill —
highest sustained speeds and strongest vertical G on the lap; descent ~11% is the
steepest downhill. Name: "Fox tube" — during 1925–27 construction a fox took refuge in
a drainage pipe here. Sources: [Circuits of the Past](https://www.circuitsofthepast.com/fuchsrohre-nurburgring/),
[nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/fuchsrohre-foxhole/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-foxhole/)

### Adenauer Forst
Fast right, then a blind, unexpectedly tight left over a crest, then right — a hidden
chicane; one of the most common tourist crash spots. Name: "Adenau Forest".
Sources: [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/adenauer-forst/),
[nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/adenauer-forst/),
[autoevolution](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/nurburgring-s-adenauer-forst-the-corner-that-makes-cars-fly-108304.html)

### Metzgesfeld
High-speed left then a slower second left, starting the long descent toward Adenau.
Field name from the family that once owned the land.
Sources: [Nordschleife erfahren](https://www.nordschleife-erfahren.de/streckenbeschreibung/metzgesfeld-und-kallenhard/),
[FanFrenzy corner list](https://www.are2007.com/nurburgring-track-guide-all-corner-names-of-the-nordschleife/)

### Kallenhard
Tight downhill right with a very late apex, opening into a long three-part right.
Named for the forested Kallenhard hill (471 m); nicknamed "Spiegelkurve" (mirror
corner) — drivers scraped mirrors on the hedges that once lined it.
Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/kallenhard/),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/kallenhard/),
[GetSpeed](https://www.getspeed-racetaxi.de/en/blog-artikel/the-most-famous-corners-on-the-nurburgring----and-their-stories)

### Wehrseifen
Very tight downhill left-right — the slowest corner on the Nordschleife — at the end
of the descent toward Adenau. Name from local topography ("Seifen" = wet stream
hollow). Sources: [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/wehrseifen-and-exmuehle/)

### Breidscheid / Adenauer Brücke
Fast downhill left kink at the bridge at Breidscheid — the lowest point of the circuit
(~320 m). Named after the Breidscheid district of Adenau.
Sources: [Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/nordschleife/gradient-of-altitude/),
[Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-7-video-2/)

### Ex-Mühle
Steep uphill right immediately after the bridge, starting the climb out of the Adenau
valley. Name: "former mill" — a water mill once stood beside the track.
Sources: [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-nordschleife-bergwerk/),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/wehrseifen-and-exmuehle/)

### Lauda-Links (unofficial name)
Fast left kink over a crest between Ex-Mühle and Bergwerk (~km 10.4). Verified
location of Niki Lauda's fiery crash, 1 August 1976 German GP — his Ferrari 312T2
snapped right here (not at Ex-Mühle or Bergwerk), hit the bank and burst into flames;
Edwards, Lunger, Ertl and Merzario pulled him out. The crash ended F1 on the
Nordschleife. Sources: [nring.info — Lauda-Links](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/lauda-links-extal/),
[Wikipedia — 1976 German Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_German_Grand_Prix),
[Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/forty-years-ago-f1-drivers-saved-niki-lauda-from-a-fier-1784656102/)

### Bergwerk
Slow-medium right that launches the ~3 km uphill drive to the Karussell — regularly
called the most important corner for lap time. Name: "Mine" — a lead-and-silver ore
mine operated nearby until the early 1900s.
Sources: [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-nordschleife-bergwerk/),
[GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/what-is-the-most-important-turn-on-the-nurburgring-poll-bergwerk-leads.327664/)

### Kesselchen
Long, very fast uphill valley section. Name: "little kettle/basin".
Sources: [Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/nordschleife/sections/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)

### Klostertal
Fast valley sweepers ending in a tight right before the Karussell climb. Name:
"monastery valley" — a 14th-century Order of St. John community occupied the valley.
Sources: [Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/klostertal/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)

### Steilstrecke (junction; not part of the racing lap)
Disused 1927 concrete "steep section" branching off between Klostertal and the
Karussell climb — 450 m ramp, gradient up to 27%, used historically for hill-start
testing. Sources: [Circuits of the Past](https://www.circuitsofthepast.com/steilstrecke-nurburgring/),
[Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-9-video-2/)

### Caracciola-Karussell
Banked concrete "bowl" hairpin (~210°) — drivers drop the inside wheels into the
banked concrete-slab ditch. Renamed for Rudolf Caracciola, who popularized hooking the
inside wheels into what was then a drainage ditch. One of only three corners ever
renamed. Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracciola_Karussell),
[BridgeToGantry](https://www.bridgetogantry.com/the-truth-behind-the-nurburgrings-karussell/),
[Porsche](https://www.porsche.com/stories/mobility/7-of-the-greatest-race-track-corners/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-carousel/)

### Hohe Acht
Uphill right at (per most sources) the highest point of the track (~616.8 m), after a
climb quoted at up to 17–18%. Named after the Hohe Acht mountain (747 m), highest peak
in the Eifel. Sources: [Wikipedia — Hohe Acht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hohe_Acht),
[nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/hohe-acht/),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/nordschleife/gradient-of-altitude/)

### Hedwigshöhe
Fast right/crest on the ridge after Hohe Acht. Named for Hedwig Creutz, wife of
Dr. Otto Creutz, driving force behind the Nürburgring's construction.
Sources: [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/),
[FanFrenzy](https://www.are2007.com/nurburgring-track-guide-all-corner-names-of-the-nordschleife/)

### Wippermann
Bumpy, cambered left-right-left — rhythm-critical, notoriously slippery kerbs. A local
family name. Sources: [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/),
[nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/)

### Eschbach
Right-hander after Wippermann dropping downhill. Named for a small stream piped to
supply a nearby village. Sources: [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/),
[Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-12-car-v-bike/)

### Brünnchen
Two rights — downhill entry, uphill exit — a natural amphitheatre; the most famous
spectator spot, nicknamed "YouTube corner". Name: "little well/spring".
Sources: [Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-brunchen/),
[nurburgringnow](https://www.nurburgringnow.com/viewing-points)

### Eiskurve
Left toward a concealed crest after Brünnchen. Name: "ice curve" — shaded by trees, it
stays damp/icy long after the rest of the track has dried.
Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/eiskurve/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)

### Pflanzgarten 1
Very fast downhill with a jump into a left — among the most dangerous parts of the
lap. Name: "planting garden" — a tree nursery once stood here. Fatal accident of Peter
Collins (Ferrari), 1958 German GP.
Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/pflanzgarten/),
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%BCrburgring),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/pflanzgarten/)

### Pflanzgarten 2 / Sprunghügel
Continuation with a second, bigger jump — the Sprunghügel ("jump hill") — taken flat
by the brave. Stefan Bellof crashed his Porsche 956 here at ~257 km/h while leading
the 1983 Nürburgring 1000 km (unhurt).
Sources: [Oversteer48 — jumps](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-jump/),
[Speedweek](https://www.speedweek.com/amp/sportwagen/news/208294/Stefan-Bellof-Rekordrunde-und-Rekord-Crash.html?lang=en),
[Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-13-panos/)

### Stefan-Bellof-S
Very fast left-right combination between the Pflanzgarten jump and Schwalbenschwanz.
Officially dedicated August 2013 (Oldtimer GP) to Stefan Bellof, honoring his 6:11.13
lap. One of the very few renamings in track history.
Sources: [ADAC NLS (2013)](https://www.nuerburgring-langstrecken-serie.de/en/2013/08/11/track-section-at-the-nordschleife-dedicated-to-stefan-bellof/),
[Wikipedia — Stefan Bellof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Bellof),
[Porsche Newsroom](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/history/porsche-stefan-georg-bellof-brothers-956-nuerburgring-nordschleife-6-11-13-minutes-legendary-record-16022.html),
[nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/stefan-bellof-s/)

### Schwalbenschwanz
Fast right, short straight, then the downhill left-right "tail". Name: "swallow's
tail" — construction workers thought the track's shape here resembled one.
Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/schwalbenschwanz/),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/stefan-bellof-s-and-schwalbenschwanz/)

### Kleines Karussell
Second banked concrete bowl — ~90° right, faster and slightly less steeply banked than
the big Karussell. Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/kleines-karussel/),
[Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-14-panos/)

### Galgenkopf
Long, partially blind right — exit speed dictates speed down the entire straight.
Name: "gallows head" — site of the Counts of Nürburg's gallows.
Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/galgenkopf/),
[Nordschleife-BtG](https://www.nordschleife-btg.net/racing-line/galgenkopf/)

### Döttinger Höhe
The Nordschleife's longest "straight" (~2.1 km, gently curving) — maximum speed
(300+ km/h for top machinery); tourist-drive entrance here. Named for the high ground
near Döttingen. Sources: [Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-15-panos/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)

### Antoniusbuche
Very fast right kink near top speed where the track passes under a bridge. Name:
"Anthony's beech" — a huge beech stood trackside until 1935; legend says a shrine to
St. Antonius stood beneath it. Sources: [nring.info](https://nring.info/nurburgring-nordschleife-corners/antoniusbuche/),
[Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-1-car-v-bike/)

### Tiergarten
Fast downhill left-right sweepers after Antoniusbuche — where record laps are won or
lost before braking for Hohenrain. Name: "animal garden / deer park".
Sources: [Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-1-drone/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)

### Hohenrain
Hard-braking right-left chicane leading to the T13 grandstand section and the junction
back toward the GP circuit. Name: "high ridge/field boundary".
Sources: [Tarmac Timewarp](https://tarmac-timewarp.com/section-1-panos/),
[Oversteer48](https://oversteer48.com/nurburgring-corner-names/)

## Caveats

- Several primary pages (Wikipedia, nuerburgring.de, RSRNurburg) could not be fetched
  directly from the build environment; content is represented via search-result
  extracts with the original URLs cited.
- Folk etymologies vary between track guides; where an official/geographic origin
  exists it is preferred.
- The Lauda 1976 crash location is confirmed as the left kink between Ex-Mühle and
  Bergwerk (~km 10.4), unofficially "Lauda-Links".
